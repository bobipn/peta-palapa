## Flow

1. **1945:** 27 September 1945, AMPTT takes over the PTT Head Office in Bandung and raises the Merah Putih.
2. **81:** "81 tahun kemudian"
3. **Terhubung:** 17 regencies · ±3,102 km of fibre · 17 service cities · 10 interconnection cities · DWDM 100 Gbps (expandable) per project
4. **Tumbuh:** operators, local ISPs, industry, BUMDes, public services and communities in 3T areas
5. **Terjaga:** in operation since 21 December 2018, managed by PT LTI
6. **Closing:** Selamat Hari Bhakti Postel ke-81 · the LTI logo · "Semua Berhak Terhubung"
